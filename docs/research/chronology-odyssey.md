## Bibliography

1. Heubeck/S. West/Hainsworth/Hoekstra/Russo/Fernández-Galiano, *A
   Commentary on Homer's Odyssey*, 3 vols., Oxford 1988–92. IN
   COPYRIGHT. **GAP: the standard day-scheme reference; content not
   extractable via open web — needs library/Google-Books manual pass.**
2. Stanford, *The Odyssey of Homer*, 2nd ed., Macmillan 1958–59. IN
   COPYRIGHT. Same gap.
3. Delebecque, *Construction de l'Odyssée*, Belles Lettres 1980 (and
   *Télémaque…*, 1958). IN COPYRIGHT. Via Lenaerts's review (AC 52
   [1983] 297–300): opens with "Les jours actuels"; ~40-day frame;
   **possibly places the Apologoi telling on Day 33 evening (one-day
   offset from our Day 34) — secondary report, needs primary check.**
   Also argues the original poem ends at 23.296 (Book 24 an addition)
   — bears on whether Day 41 is "authentic" timeline.
4. M.L. West, *The Making of the Odyssey*, OUP 2014. IN COPYRIGHT
   (scholarship usable; his editorial text remains banned site-side).
   Chronology treatment unconfirmed via open web.
5. de Jong, *A Narratological Commentary on the Odyssey*, CUP 2001. IN
   COPYRIGHT. Via Scodel's BMCR review: flags Athena's Ithaca→Sparta
   trip spanning end of Bk 13 to start of Bk 15 as a chronological
   oddity (soft spot in every day-count, ours included).
6. Baikouzis & Magnasco, "Is an eclipse described in the Odyssey?",
   PNAS 105.26 (2008) 8823–28. OPEN ACCESS (PMC2440358). Contains an
   actual day-numbered chronology table (backward count from the
   slaying = Day 0 ≈ −40); builds sequential AND parallel numberings to
   handle simultaneity.
7. Zieliński, "Die Behandlung gleichzeitiger Ereignisse im antiken
   Epos", Philologus Suppl. 8 (1901) 405–49. PD. Founding statement of
   Zielinski's Law (simultaneous events narrated sequentially) — the
   axis for dating the Telemachy vs the Ogygia strand. Exact wording
   NOT yet verified against the German.
8. Carbon, *Zieliński's Law and Its Validity*, MA thesis, McMaster
   (MacSphere 11375/12086). Open repository.
9. Merry–Riddell, *Homer's Odyssey* I (Books I–XII), Oxford 1886. PD.
   archive.org + Perseus, line-browsable.
10. **Monro**, *Homer's Odyssey, Books XIII–XXIV*, Oxford 1901. PD.
    **Best PD find: labels narrative days in his notes.**
11. Gilbert Murray, *The Rise of the Greek Epic*, 3rd ed., Oxford
    1924. PD.

## Key corroborations and disagreements vs our Day 1–41 calendar

- **MATCH (PD, quotable): Monro at Od. 13.18: "Morning of the 34th day
  of the action of the poem"; at 13.93: "the dawn of the 35th day."**
  Our Book 13 = Days 34–35 with the same boundary. Independent
  corroboration of our frame numbering.
- Delebecque: telling possibly Day 33 evening (one-day offset) —
  UNVERIFIED (secondary). Check before treating as variant.
- Book 24 authenticity (Delebecque: poem ends 23.296) → whether Day 41
  is "authentic"; contested-scope class → John's gate if surfaced.
- Zielinski simultaneity: Telemachy (Days 2–6) vs Ogygia strand
  parallelism; second divine council (Bk 5) as replay-vs-event.
  Baikouzis & Magnasco's dual numbering is the honest display model.
- NON-disagreement (flagged so nobody invents one): Book 5's spans are
  poem-stated and undisputed — raft 4 days, 17 days sailing
  (5.278–79), 2 nights adrift (5.313), ashore day 3.

## Poem-internal durations (for the analepsis apparatus)

- Circe: one year — Od. 10.469–474 (ἐνιαυτός; verified via DCC).
- Calypso: seven years — Od. 7.253–260 (Odysseus's own statement).
