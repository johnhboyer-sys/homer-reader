# Paywalled-access pull queue (2026-07-29)

Consolidated from the five Grok-verified research dossiers. Ordered by leverage:
what each item settles, and which dossier consumes it. John logs into
JSTOR/university resources in Chrome; the orchestrator pulls; excerpts go back
to the dossier that asked. Full texts land in the gitignored `research-cache/`
only — never committed, never republished.

## Tier 1 — each settles a load-bearing claim

1. **Hainsworth on Il. 12.118–19** (Cambridge *Iliad* Commentary vol. III).
   Confirms or breaks the left/right orientation frame the whole schematic
   sheet hangs on — and the Clay constant-orientation thesis now resting on a
   BMCR review. → POEM-TOPOGRAPHY §3.1, §8.
2. **Kraft, Rapp, Kayan & Luce, *Geology* 31.2 (2003): 163–66 — the figures.**
   The abstract settles nothing; the figures carry the harbour/bay geometry.
   → PALEOGEOGRAPHY §5 item 2.
3. **Kayan 2014, *Studia Troica* Monographien 5, 694–727, esp. fig. 8** (his
   plotted sea-level curve) — the single most useful figure for barrier and
   lagoon. → PALEOGEOGRAPHY §5 item 5.
4. **Janko on Il. 13.681 and 16.286** (Cambridge vol. IV). Settles C-3
   (Protesilaus's ship). → POEM-TOPOGRAPHY §8.
5. **Richardson on Il. 22.145–56** (Cambridge vol. VI). The chase-route
   complex: springs, washing-troughs, the circuit. → POEM-TOPOGRAPHY §8.
6. **Edwards, *The Iliad: A Commentary* vol. V, pp. 200–209** + notes on
   18.481, 497–508, 509, 604–5. Two Shield claims are currently second-hand
   through Schmiel's BMCR review. → SHIELD §7.
7. **Cook, *The Troad* (1973)** — the identification backbone; not one page
   was reachable, so no Cook page number exists in any dossier. Esp. the
   Callicolone coordinate (is it Cook's?), p. 167 (Kesik), and the site
   entries for every `traditional` tier. → TROAD-TOPOGRAPHY §6; PALEOGEOGRAPHY.
