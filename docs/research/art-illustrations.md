## Doctrine notes

- PD-Art/Bridgeman covers faithful 2D repro of 2D works (Flaxman scans
  safe). Photos of 3D objects (vases, sculpture) carry the
  photographer's copyright — use Commons files with explicit CC tags,
  credit the photographer, note the holding museum.
- Epic-cycle vs Homeric-text: Penthesilea (Aethiopis), Trojan Horse
  imagery (Little Iliad/Sack), Peleus-Thetis wedding (Cypria) are NOT
  narrated in our texts — label as epic-cycle/traditional per the
  tradition-naming rule, never pin to a vulgate line.
