# Resource survey — features worth adapting in our style (2026-07-17)

Sonnet research lane; licensing-first. Rule: we adapt FEATURE IDEAS from
PD/open sources or pure computation — never content or design from
in-copyright resources. Full reasoning in the session log; this is the
decision record.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **Computed hexameter scansion** — auto-scan every line (dactyl/
   spondee, elision, synizesis) from our own Greek; overlay toggle with
   confidence flags on ambiguous resolutions. Pure computation, zero
   licensing. Effort M. Learners high, scholars spot-check.
2. **Chamberlain Homeric audio** (hypotactic.com; CC-BY) — line-synced
   reconstructed-pronunciation recitation; full Iliad + Od. 1–7 exist.
   Per-line/book "hear it," attributed, graceful degrade where
   unrecorded. Effort S–M. Learners + new readers.
3. **Formula/phrase tracking search** (Chicago Homer pattern; feature
   only — their English is Lattimore, forbidden) — "find this exact
   phrase everywhere" over our own corpus + formula index. M–L.
4. **Character network graph** — co-occurrence/speech/kinship force
   graph from characters.json + DICES, certainty-tiered. Pure
   presentation on existing data. M. The shareable artifact.
5. **Place-anchored mentions panel** (ToposText pattern) — "every
   mention of this place in both epics" off existing places data. S–M.
6. **Homer Multitext scholia** (CC-BY-SA; ShareAlike homework) — real
   scholiast commentary paired with our already-ingested Allen sigla
   (1,789 diplai / 411 obeloi / 290 dotted / 69 asterisks, currently
   inert data). L. The scholar differentiator; natural fast-follow to
   the Loeb-notes machinery.
7. **Benner's annotated Iliad selections (1903, PD)** — learner's-notes
   layer on marquee passages, attributed, draft-badged. M.
8. **Pharr 1920 (FIRST ed. only — 1985 revision NOT PD) + Monro's
   Homeric Grammar (PD)** — "why does this form look odd" dialect notes
   inside the parse popup. M.
9. **KWIC concordance** — computed from our corpus (Prendergast/Dunbar
   pattern; their 1962 revised reprint is in-copyright — build fresh). M.
10. **Apologoi frame-timeline** ("mise en abyme" strip) — narrator-layer
    visualization; doubles as the visual aid for review-queue item 0a. M.
11. **Per-book vocabulary lists** (Perseus vocab pattern) — "words to
    know before Book N" from our lemma data. S.
12. **CTS-anchored commentary widget** (Scaife pattern) — future
    extensibility only; not now.

## Considered and rejected

theoi.com (copyrighted prose; external link only) · Lombardo audio (NOT
PD) · Hestia (Herodotus-specific; redundant) · Pelagios/Peripleo
(infrastructure overkill) · Loebolus (bulk scans; we did the narrower
better thing) · Eustathius (critical edition in copyright to ~2030) ·
Geneva scholia papyri (image-based, niche) · WordHoard (overlaps our
search/formula tooling) · LLM knowledge graphs (no stable dataset).
