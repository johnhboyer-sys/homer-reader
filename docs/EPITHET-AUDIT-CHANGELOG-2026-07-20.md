# Epithet extraction audit — proposed change log (2026-07-20)

Status: **awaiting John’s approval; no data rewrite has been performed.**

## Scope and method

This audit covers every current formula record in [Iliad epithets](../build/dist/iliad/epithets.json) and [Odyssey epithets](../build/dist/odyssey/epithets.json): 873 Iliad records across 50 indexed entities and 404 Odyssey records across 37 indexed entities, 1,277 records total. It compares the stored Greek and reviewed English gloss against the indexed person, lemma sequence, every stored vulgate reference, and raw verse in `build/dist/{iliad,odyssey}/book-NN.json`. Vulgate lineation is untouched.

The detector is a repeated 2–6-token name-containing n-gram collector, not a grammatical epithet classifier. The audit therefore rejects strings that merely bind a name to particles, conjunctions, articles, pronouns, prepositions, verbs, objects, possessive/kinship syntax, neighboring names, or descriptors belonging to somebody else. Genuine epithets, patronymics, titles, inflected descriptors, and standard speech/narrative formulas that include a genuine descriptor are retained.

Totals: **850 proposed candidates** (Iliad 625: 565 certain, 58 probable contaminated cores, 2 human-scope; Odyssey 225 certain) and **427 retained** (Iliad 248; Odyssey 179). The Odyssey ledger’s existing display classifier independently identifies 102 pure connective entries; the additional 123 failures are syntactic, relational, locative, verbal, or cross-entity spill.

Integrity checks: all 404 Odyssey records and 2,814 Odyssey refs were traversed; 0 missing glosses, 0 count/ref mismatches, 0 duplicate entity+text pairs, and 0 nonexistent raw-line refs. The Iliad audit likewise traversed all 873 records and recorded a raw first-line context for every candidate.

## Pattern codes

- **P1** — name/ethnonym plus connective or function word only.
- **P2** — governed prepositional or locative phrase, not a descriptor.
- **P3** — action, predicate, object, possession, kinship, or other syntactic mention.
- **P4** — genuine epithet/title embedded in clause or connective spill; correct to the clean core if approved.
- **P5** — list or neighboring descriptor belongs to another entity.
- **P6** — Ares martial metonymy/periphrasis; scope decision required.
- **P7** — truncated genuine title; correct rather than simply delete.
