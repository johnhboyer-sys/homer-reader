Dictionary (LSJ)

ὅθι, relat. Adv., poet. for οὗ, A. where, Il. 2.722, Od. 14.73, 397, al. ; also ὅ. περ Il. 2.861, al., Pi. Fr. 77: used by Trag. only in lyr., exc. S. El. 709 : rare in Prose, Pl. Thg. 125a; ὅ. περ Id. Phd. 108b ; found in Arc. Prose, IG 5(2).16.12(Tegea, iii B. C.). [In Hom.ι is freq. elided ; and so S.l.c. (s.v.l.).]
