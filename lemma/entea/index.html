Dictionary (LSJ)

ἔντεα, τά, fighting gear, arms, armour, ἔ. ἀρήϊα Il. 10.407, Od. 23.368; ἔ. πατρός 19.17; esp. coat of mail, corslet, Il. 10.34; ἔντεʼ ἔδυνεν 3.339, etc. II. furniture, appliances, tackle, ἔ. δαιτός Od. 7.232; ἔ. νηός rigging, h.Ap. 489, Pi. N. 4.70; ἔ. ἵππεια trappings, harness, ib. 9.22, cf. P. 4.235; ἔντη δίφρου harness, A. Pers. 194 (but ἔντεα alone for chariots, Pi. O. 4.24); ἔντεα αὐλῶν periphr. for αὐλοί, ib. 7.12; also ἔντεα alone, musical instruments, Id. P. 12.21; of the instruments of the Γάλλαι, Lyr.Adesp. 121; ἔντεα Φοίβου Call. Ap. 19.—Ep. and Lyr. word, once in Trag. (v. supr.):—sg. ἔντος only in Archil. 6.
