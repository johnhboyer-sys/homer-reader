Dictionary (LSJ)

δρυμός, ὁ, copse, thicket, S. OT 1399, SIG 57.28 (Milet., v. B.C.), E. Hipp. 1127 (lyr.), Tab.Heracl. 1.19, PLille 5.13 (iii B. C.), LXX Ec. 2.6, Plb. 2.15.2, AP 7.544, etc.: pl., A. Fr. 304.10, Theoc. 1.117, AP 6.13 (Leon.), 9.4 (Cyllen.), 84 (Antiphan.), Plu. Comp.Per.Fab. 1. II. Hom. has only pl., δρῠμά, τά, Il. 11.118, Od. 10.150, 197, 251, also in Simm. 15 (prob.); δρῡμά in late Ep., D.P. 492, Opp. C. 1.64. III. δρυμός· φρούριον, Hsch., perh. in this sense in PPetr. 2p.140. (Cf. Skt. drumá- ‘tree’, Slav. drǔmǔ ‘thicketʼ: ῠ is original, ῡ borrowed from δρῦς.)
