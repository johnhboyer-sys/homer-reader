Dictionary (LSJ)

Λοκροί, οἱ, A. the Locrians, of whom there were three tribes, the Opuntian, opposite Euboea, Il. 2.527, Th. 1.108, Str. 9.3.1,9.3.17; the Epicnemidian, on Mt. Cnemis on the Maliac Gulf, Id.ll.cc.; and the Ozolian, on the Corinthian Gulf, Th. 1.5, 103, etc.: the Epizephyrian or Zephyrian were a colony of the last on Mt. Zephyrium in lower Italy, Pi. O. 10(11).13, Th. 4.24 sq.,7.1, etc.: prov. Λοκρῶν σύνθημα, of deceit, Eust. 275.43, Hsch., Suid.:—Adj. Λοκρός, ά, όν, Locrian, Lyc. 1429:—also Λοκρικός, ή, όν, Poll. 4.65, etc.:—fem. Λοκρίς, ίδος, Pi. P. 2.19; ἡ Λ. (sc. γῆ) Ar. Av. 152, etc.
