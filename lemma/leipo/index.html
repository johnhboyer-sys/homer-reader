Pers. 984 (lyr.), etc.: pf. λέλοιπα Od. 14.134: plpf. ἐλελοίπειν (Att. -η) X. Cyr. 2.1.21:—Med., in prop. sense chiefly in compds.: aor. 2 ἐλιπόμην Hdt. 1.186, 2.40, E.
