Sc. 62 ; ἀγκῶνι νύξας having nudged him with the elbow, Od. 14.485, cf. Theoc. 21.50, Plu. Profect. 2.79e, etc. ; γνωμιδίῳ γνώμην ν. prick it (and see what is in it), Ar.
