Dictionary (LSJ)

Ἴλιος [ῑλ], ου, ἡ, Ilios or A. Ilium, the city of Ilus, Troy, Il. 5.210, al., Alc. Supp. 8.4, E. Andr. 103 (eleg.): Ἴλιον, τό, Hom. only in Il. 15.71, but always in Trag. (exc. E.l.c.):—hence Ep. genitives, Ἰλιόθεν, from Troy, Il. 14.251, Od. 9.39; Ἰλιόθι πρό before Troy, 8.581, etc.; Ἰλιόφι κλυτὰ τείχεα the walls of Troy, Il. 21.295. II. as Adj., Ἴλιος, α, ον, Ilian, Trojan, Ἀθάνα E. Hec. 1008: -ος, ον is f.l. in Id. Hel. 1164 (lyr.).
