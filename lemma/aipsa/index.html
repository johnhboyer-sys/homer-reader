Dictionary (LSJ)

αἶψα (Boeot. ἦψα prob. in Corinn. BKT 5(2).36), Adv.
quick, forthwith, on a sudden, freq. in Hom. (also αἶψα μάλα, αἶψα δʼ ἔπειτα, Il. 4.70, Od. 15.193), cf. Sapph. 1.13, Thgn. 663, Sol. 2, Pi. P. 4.133, Emp. 35.14, A. Supp. 481.—Poet., exc. in Mon.Ant. 18.322 (Gortyn).
