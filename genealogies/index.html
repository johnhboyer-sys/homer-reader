Genealogies

The four family lines that structure the poems: the House of Atreus (Agamemnon and Menelaus), the House of Aeacus (Achilles), the House of Troy (Priam and the Dardanid kings), and the Olympian gods. Every member below comes from this edition’s character apparatus; dashed connectors are identifications the Homeric poems themselves don’t make — links added by later tradition.
